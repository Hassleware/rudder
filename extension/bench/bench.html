Filter benchmark

Scores the filter the viewer uses (probe.js: probe on every word, Gemma judging only unsure sentences, one ensemble score) on 10 filters: 5 the method was chosen on, and 5 held out. A passage "fires" if any of its sentences is blocked. AUROC = on-topic passages ranked above off-topic ones by the cascade's score (1 = perfect, 0.5 = guessing). Uses the GPU for about three minutes and clears the chat's memory (the next message re-reads the chat). The search behind the method is research/probe-research.mjs.
